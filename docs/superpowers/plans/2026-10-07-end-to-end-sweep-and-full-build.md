## Review focus

1. Late upload/provider callbacks cannot mutate a stopped or subsequent sweep; restart cannot pretend in-flight work survived (Phases 1–2).
2. Repeated preview timestamps are recorded/skipped; stationary identical JPEGs with advancing timestamps are not proof of a frozen sensor (Phase 1).
3. A model proposal count is not automatically physical book count; repeated views and identical-title copies must not duplicate/merge silently (Phase 2).
4. Country and currency are independent: requesting USD in the UAE does not mean searching US listings, and cache entries cannot leak between locales/conditions (Phase 3).
5. Source currencies and converted values never mix in totals; unsupported FX, stale/ambiguous offers, appraisal and broken evidence are not filled or silently valued at zero (Phases 3–5).

## Phase 3 — Locale pricing contract (mandatory, not an AED-only adapter)

Public interface: `price_book(identity: BookIdentity, locale: Locale, condition_assumed: str) -> BookValuation`. `Locale` has independent country and currency fields. Country selects the target market; currency selects output denomination. The same pipeline handles all configured markets and reports unsupported markets/currencies rather than quietly choosing UAE/AED. AE/AED and US/USD are required concrete demonstrations; AE/USD additionally exercises separation of market and denomination.

- Replacement and used quotes are retrieved/validated independently. Search/discovery and full-page extraction are separate stages; preserve URL, timestamp, raw source evidence, identity/format/edition-equivalence, stock, condition, amount and actual source currency.
- Prefer target-market sources. A local quote in another denomination still requires conversion to requested currency. If only a defensible foreign-market quote exists, record original market/source/amount/currency, dated FX source/rate, resulting currency and converted=true. A missing FX rate leaves output amount null; never merely relabel a currency.
- Reuse the guarded Amazon.ae adapter where applicable; general extraction must not hardcode AED, `.ae` domains or five owner titles. Never infer used prices as a discount from new prices, consume unrelated variant/bundle prices, bypass access restrictions or accept model-memory prices.
- Cache keys include identity/edition or equivalent selection, target country, output currency and new/used condition. Keep quote freshness/FX date visible. A quote for one country/condition cannot satisfy another's request by accident.
- Ordinary identified books only for book pricing. Rare/signed/antiquarian/original-art automatic prices are suppressed. Approved candidate-price appraisal routing threshold is AED1,000 equivalent, using defensible FX; no guessed conversion may determine it.
- Price recognizable non-books with retrievable local replacement listings; an unknown brand/model gets only a sourced range. Original or unclear artwork remains `needs_appraisal` unless the user establishes a print, with that assertion retained; missing sources leave amounts null. Test non-book source/date/currency and compare category recall against the independent item list, not merely the books.
- Lead authors source/discovery prompts, source admission, rate provider/rounding/freshness rules and exact fixtures before execution. Preserve inaccessible/no-offer outcomes and coverage separately from price accuracy.
